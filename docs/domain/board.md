# Board

**Purpose.** The web adapter: the Future City Builder page. It draws the site, turns clicks into layout commands or crews' orders, words every event, check, issue and notice, and shows the score, the line checks, the stretch tests and the clock. Zoomed into a tile, it draws that tile's ground from the Ground's plan, one level at a time. It holds no rules.

**Package.** `apps/board` (`@city/board`, Vite). Depends on `@city/site`, `@city/run` and `@city/ground` (and `@city/ledger` through them). `pnpm --filter @city/board build` emits one self-contained page at `apps/board/dist/index.html`, so it can still be published the way the sketch was.

## What the Board owns

| Concern | Module | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Every sentence | `src/wording.ts` | Worded from typed facts and events (ADR-0004). Tested against the sketch's recorded sentences. |
| The line cursor | `src/moves.ts` | Where the mover line continues from after a click. A click with a tool becomes a Site command, or a Run order while the clock runs. |
| The session | `src/session.ts` | The game, the tool in hand, the tile picked, the stretch test shown, the view (the map, or one tile's ground at a level; never saved), the history undo keeps. On a clock, the board as checked for the people living there (`livingOf`), what holds the rest back, the stops under construction (`stopsBuilding`) and the work in hand in the crew order. |
| Storage | `src/storage.ts` | A saved game behind one interface. The browser store keeps the sketch's key and shape, so a city saved from the published page still loads. |
| Drawing | `src/draw/*` | The board (SVG), the tray, the score, the clock card and the work in hand, the inspector, the rules and the stretch tests. |
| A tile's ground | `src/draw/ground.ts` | The Ground's plan of a neighborhood or the ring, one SVG unit to the metre, with a legend, a scale bar and the placements the ledger did not fix; the view bar with the way back and the level switch. Rows, entries and buildings carry their counts as data attributes so a flow can add them up. |
| The text export | `src/export.ts` | "Copy the city as text", for pasting back into a conversation. Carries a MONEY section: the layers, every row with its mark, and the run's spend so far. |
| What the city needs | `src/draw/needs.ts`, `accept` in `src/moves.ts` | The Site's proposals for the layout (ADR-0008), one line each with its price, Show (marks its tiles) and Add, and Add all. Building goes through the Site's `applyProposal` and the ordinary commit, so undo and saving work as for any move. Holds no rules: the Board never decides what a city needs or where it goes. |
| The site from the air | `src/draw/angled/*` (`projection.ts`, `extrude.ts`, `scene.ts`, `canvas.ts`, `view.ts`), the "From the air" button in `src/main.ts` | An angled view of the whole site on a canvas, beside the map: every neighborhood, ring building and plant is a block with its stories, every stretch of line a strip, the Ground's shapes in metres on the map's hex lattice (`sceneOf`), projected under a camera (yaw, pitch, scale, origin) and extruded into the faces that face the viewer, shaded by one light, painted far to near. Far, a neighborhood is one block the size of its tile; from a quarter pixel a metre it is its rows of townhouses. The view bar says what was painted; the canvas carries `data-blocks` and `data-stories`. Canvas 2D, no library (ADR-0014). |
| The month clock | `src/draw/clock.ts`, `src/autorun.ts`, the Play, Next month and Next year buttons in `src/main.ts` | A run starts with the Run's month clock (ADR-0009) unless "Year by year, as the sketch runs it" is checked. The card says where the clock stands and who lives there (`peopleNow`), Next month steps one (`monthStep`) and says the month (`monthLine`), and "The people, month by month" lists every month. Play runs the months on a timer at Slow, Steady or Fast (1 s, 400 ms, 100 ms a month), redrawing after each and saying it; Pause stops them; an order placed while they run pauses them and says so; Next month, Next year, undo, Stop and a new run pause first; the timer stops itself at year 20 and Play goes. One Play is one undo step, back to where it was pressed. The timer is in memory only, so a reloaded run opens paused in the month it reached (ADR-0013). The session checks the board for the people living there (`livingOf`), not full homes. "Who comes and goes" lists the Ledger's population premises, worded (`POPULATION_PREMISE`), each with its mark. |
| The day | `src/draw/day.ts` | "One day, hour by hour" under the stretch tests: the Site's day view (`dayOf`) for the people living here now, a sentence (`dayCaption`), the 24 hours (trips, on a mover, movers moving, power, pupils; the busiest hour in bold), and the day's shape worded (`DAY_PREMISE`) with each figure's mark. The Board computes nothing: its numbers are the Site's. |
| Money | `src/money.ts`, `src/draw/money.ts` | The Site's cost of what stands gathered by row id in layer order (`linesOf`), and the run's spend so far (`spentSoFar`); the card that shows them. The Board prices nothing: it asks `costOf` and `totalSpend` and words the layers, rows, premises and marks. |

The page's HTML and styles are the sketch's two halves, plus the view bar and the ground's styles added for the physical city.

## Zooming in

"Look closer" in the inspector, on a neighborhood or a ring, shows that tile's ground in place of the map; the tile map is the zoomed-out view and does not change. The view bar names the tile, switches between the surface and the sub level, and goes back. The view lives in the session only: a reload opens on the map, and a tile that loses its ground (erased, undone, the site cleared) sends the view back to the map on the next redraw. The plan is Ground's; the Board picks a level with `layersAt` and words the caption, the legend and the placeholder note.

## Opening cities

"Load the ledger's city" puts the sketch's 50,000 on the board; "Load it sized to its people" puts the same city with every ring building and reactor module it does not need taken away (`sizedLayout`, ADR-0008): 12.2 billion to build against 13.4. Either stops the clock, and Undo brings the old city back.

## What the city needs

The card under the inspector lists what the layout lacks to keep its lines whole, in the order the Site proposes it, each with what it adds to the bill (a school or fields nearer home can cost less, since fewer people ride). Show marks the tiles a proposal touches; Add builds it, with any plant or ring proposed earlier that its line runs to; Add all builds every one. A whole city reads "The city needs nothing more to keep its lines whole." While the clock runs nothing is proposed, since an order is not built yet and proposing again would order it twice; stop the clock to see what the city needs. A full board says it has no room. The size line is never on the list: it waits on people (ADR-0006, 14 of 15).

## Money

"What it costs" (lines 24 to 29, ADR-0007) sits under the stretch tests. A headline gives what stands costs to build and to run, a person and in all, with the Land and Financing lines. A table gives the six layers, the two totals, Land and Financing for this city, a person, and the car city of the same size a person (from the Score's car comparison, which carries the car column's cost). A disclosure lists every row: how many at which unit cost, the cost, and the premise's mark as a chip, **Yours**, **Sourced** with its year (the source in the chip's title), **Sourced, accepted** when David has accepted it, or **Mine**. While the clock runs the card adds what the run has spent so far, by layer and in all, and the record gains a Spent column, one year's outlay. An empty site says nothing costs anything yet; year 0 says nothing is spent yet. A row the Ledger or the Site adds without a name here, or a premise without a unit, fails the type check. The notes under the game say how spend is paid and that every premise has been accepted (2026-10-14), with water following the green ground.

## Built in order

Every run the page starts, yearly or month by month, is built in order (ADR-0012 Decision 1): the line, the ring, the plants and the schools take the crews before fields and homes, and a finished neighborhood is lived in only when the city carries it. Between steps the board is checked for the people the clock admitted, so a neighborhood standing empty reads as empty. The year and the month say who is held back, grouped by what they wait for ("2 neighborhoods stand empty, waiting for power."; "1 neighborhood holds only households with no school-age children, waiting for middle-school and high-school seats."), the inspector says it for the neighborhood picked, and the export lists it as "Held back now". Line 6 says "The mover line to them is under construction." for the neighborhoods the work in hand gives a stop, "Draw it through the other one." for any it does not, and keeps "Draw the mover line through them." when nothing in hand reaches them. A run saved before this decision restores without it and plays the sketch's way.

## The month clock and the day

A run is on the month clock unless "Year by year, as the sketch runs it" is checked, which leaves the sketch's clock as it was: homes fill the year they are finished; flows 3 and 4 check it to keep their pinned figures. On the month clock, "Start the clock on this city" and "Start a city from nothing" put the clock at year 0 with the orders open and the months still; Play lets them run (David, 2026-10-19: the sim runs month by month on its own, with visible progress). A run founded from nothing stands empty until its first neighborhoods open and the city carries them, then fills at no more than 400 people a month across the city (4,800 a year, Irvine 1980–1990): two neighborhoods ordered at year 0 with two reactor modules and a unit of each plant open in year 2 and stand empty, waiting for power; the modules open at year 3 and the first 400 move in, the childless share, since no school stands; the first month of year 4 brings them to 799 ("400 moved in, 0.4 born, 0.3 died and 1.5 moved away"), held for school seats. A month or a year passing says the month; the month clock survives a reload.

The day is a summer weekday (David, 2026-10-14). The ledger's city and the sized city, every neighborhood full: 50,000 people, 147,727 trips, 14,773 of them from 5 to 6 p.m.; power peaks at 75 MW and draws 1,440 MWh; at the busiest hour 188 of the sized city's 217 movers are moving; 7,800 pupils go to school between 7 and 8 a.m. and home between 2 and 3 p.m. An empty site's day has no trips, no power and every mover parked. A population or day premise the Ledger adds without wording here fails the type check.

## Acceptance

`apps/board/test/wording.test.ts` holds every sentence to the sketch's: 15 recorded assessments' titles, line labels, details and tile issues; 29 moves; 25 orders; every year's notices on 2 runs; the stretch tests' labels. `money.test.ts` holds the dollar formatting, the mark wording, every premise's unit and layer's name, the rows gathered by id in layer order summing the Site's rows, and the spend so far. `wording.test.ts` also holds the month clock's and the day's sentences: a month on a full city and on a bare site, where the clock stands, all 24 hours named once, the sized city's day and an empty one, and every population and day premise named and worded. `session.test.ts` covers the session and the storage adapter with the memory store: what is saved, what undo brings back, the 80-step history, a store that throws, and the view (opens on the map, looks only at ground, switches level, is never saved, returns to the map when the ground goes). `wording.test.ts` holds the sentences built in order adds: line 6 with none, some and all of its neighborhoods under construction, lived in and empty; the held-back sentences grouped; the inspector's; the year and the month built in order, and the sketch's clock saying nothing of it. `angled.test.ts` holds the camera and the extrusion to numbers (the plan view is the identity, a tilt foreshortens by the sine, a height lifts by the cosine, unproject round-trips, nearer is later; the sides facing the viewer then the top, shaded, clockwise as counter-clockwise, refusals), and `scene.test.ts` holds the scene to the Ground and the Site (the tile lattice at the Ledger's radius, a block a neighborhood far and a row mid with the Ground's row count and stories, a block a ring building with the ring's stories, a block a plant, a strip a stretch, an empty site empty; the fitted camera inside the margin; the painter handed every face far to near, coloured by kind; the session's view from the air). `autorun.test.ts` covers the months running on their own with a scheduler fired by hand, asserting on the session's game, history and store: one month step shared by the tap and the timer (the next month saved, the year said when a month closes one, the years up at the horizon; null with nothing changed off the month clock or past it), one year step, Play remembering one state and firing one month a time, no second remember or timer on a second Play, refusals on a yearly run, no clock and the horizon, Pause stopping the firing, the timer stopping itself at year 20, and a new speed re-arming without resetting the month. `session.test.ts` also covers a run built in order: the board checked for the childless share (no pupils, so the school lines wait) and everyone again off the clock, the stops under construction until the stretch opens, the work in hand in the crew order, and a unit of a service the city is short of working ahead of the stretches. `export.test.ts` holds the export built in order: the people admitted, who is held back now, line 6 under construction, and nothing held back off the clock or on the sketch's. The flows above are the browser smoke test.

## Not in the Board

Nothing here decides whether a line holds, what a trip does, or what a year brings. Those are the Site's and the Run's, and the Board asks them.
