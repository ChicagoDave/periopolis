# The lines

A **line** is one numbered premise. Everything in the city is checked against the lines. Lines 1 to 19 are worded as published in the ledger; 20 to 23 are in David's words from the conversation of 8 to 12 October 2026; 24 to 29 are the money lines (`docs/brainstorm/future-city/money.md`); 30 to 32 are the setting, a lake shore, added 2026-10-20 (`docs/brainstorm/future-city/setting.md`). All are **yours**.

| # | Line | Where it is answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vehicles drive themselves and come in any size, from one seat to a bus. | Ledger: the fleet, by class (pod, cab, shuttle). Built into every tile. |
| 2 | Nobody drives inside the city, so nothing is parked there. | Ledger: parking ground is 0; the fleet waits in a depot. Built into every tile. |
| 3 | Daily needs sit within a walk: leisure, a library, non-ER medical and fresh food. | Ledger: the center is sized by the hungriest daily need. Site: every neighborhood feeds its daily list; the city is big enough for urgent care and fine dining. |
| 4 | Parks with tennis, pickleball and basketball courts are nearby. Larger fields are a slightly longer walk or a short ride. | Ledger: a park in every center, fields shared between centers. Site: fields are a longer walk or a short ride away. |
| 5 | Nothing moves near pedestrians that can cause harm. | Ledger: the harm line and the speed a bike is under it. Site: nothing is hauled on the surface. |
| 6 | No one needs a vehicle near their door. People walk to the movers, which run on a sub level. | Ledger: stops, the walk to a stop, the mover line. Site: every neighborhood has a mover stop. |
| 7 | Freight reaches the door with a porter and a powered cart. | Ledger: porters for bulky goods. Built into every tile. |
| 8 | Bikes have segregated lanes. | Ledger: bike lanes on the edges between neighborhoods; half of bikeable trips are biked. Built into every tile. |
| 9 | Groups of houses share a single sub-level entry point. | Ledger: entries, homes per entry, the walkway from entry to stop. Built into every tile. |

| 11 | Height graduates from the outer edges to the middle. | Ledger: three stories in the fabric, 25 in the ring. Built into every tile. |

| 23 | Restrictions on paper and plastic; personal drinking containers; no commercial mail. | Not checked anywhere yet. |
